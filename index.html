Task Instructions

Words will appear in the centre of the screen one at a time.
Your job is to press the key matching the INK COLOUR of the word, ignoring what the word says.

Keep your fingers resting on the D, F, J, and K keys.
Respond as fast and accurately as possible.

Press the SPACEBAR to start 6 practice trials.

Practice Complete

You are ready for the main task. There will be 32 trials in one continuous block.
Remember to respond to the INK COLOUR as quickly and accurately as you can.

Press the SPACEBAR to start the real test.

Thank you for participating!

The goal of this test is to actually measure the Stroop Effect, which is the delay in reaction time that happens when your brain is forced to process the conflict between the word meaning and the ink colour. Specifically, we want to see whether conflicting English words cause a bigger delay for native English speakers than for students who have lived in an English-speaking country for 3 years or less.

We could not tell you that we were comparing language backgrounds before the test because knowing that might have made you self-conscious during the test and could change how you performed.

Having delays in reaction or making mistakes on mismatched words is completely normal and happens to almost everyone. This test is just to see how brains handle conflicting information and is not an intelligence test. Your results are anonymous and are NOT SAVED in this demo version.
